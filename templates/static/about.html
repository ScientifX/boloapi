About Scientifics.io

Who We Are

Scientifics.io provides comprehensive, developer-friendly access to FBI Most Wanted data through our specialized API platform. We're a team of data engineers and API developers who believe that critical public safety information should be accessible, searchable, and easy to integrate into applications that serve the public good.

We built this platform because accessing FBI wanted persons data shouldn't require manual web scraping or navigating complex government websites. Whether you're building a safety app, conducting research, or working in law enforcement, you need reliable, structured data delivered through modern APIs.

What We Do

We transform raw FBI Most Wanted data into clean, normalized, searchable records that you can access via simple REST APIs. Our platform handles the complex work of:

  • Data Extraction: Automated daily pulls from FBI sources
  • Data Cleaning: Normalization, validation, and enrichment of records
  • Historical Tracking: Maintaining change history and trend analysis
  • Search Infrastructure: Powerful search with boolean logic and filters
  • Developer Experience: Clean JSON responses, comprehensive documentation, JWT authentication

Who We Serve

Law Enforcement & Security

Quick access to wanted persons data for threat assessment, background checks, and coordination across jurisdictions. Our API integrates seamlessly with existing security systems and databases.

Media & Journalism

Reliable data feeds for news organizations covering crime, public safety, and law enforcement stories. Access historical data to identify trends and patterns in wanted persons cases.

Researchers & Academics

Clean, structured datasets for criminology research, policy analysis, and academic studies. Our historical data enables longitudinal studies and trend analysis.

Technology Companies

Build public safety features into your applications. From background check services to community safety apps, our API provides the data foundation you need with enterprise-grade reliability.

Our Approach

We're service-oriented first. That means:

  • Reliability: 99.9% uptime with automated monitoring and failover
  • Data Quality: Extensive validation and cleaning processes ensure accuracy
  • Developer-Friendly: RESTful APIs, clear documentation, helpful error messages
  • Responsive Support: Real humans who understand your integration challenges
  • Transparent Pricing: Clear tiers based on your needs, no hidden fees

Data Source & Accuracy

Our data comes directly from the FBI's public Wanted API. We refresh data daily and maintain both current and historical records. While we take extensive measures to ensure data accuracy through validation and cleaning processes, we always recommend verifying critical information directly with law enforcement agencies.

We're data providers, not law enforcement. Our role is to make public information more accessible and useful for legitimate applications.
