Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2025

At Scientifics.io, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our FBI Wanted Persons API service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used for account identification, activation, and communication
  • API Key: Generated automatically and stored as a secure hash
  • Account Preferences: Service tier, notification settings (if any)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our API, we automatically collect:

  • API Usage Data: Endpoints accessed, query parameters, response times
  • IP Addresses: For rate limiting and security purposes
  • Timestamps: When requests are made
  • User Agent: Browser/application information
  • Authentication Data: Token validation and expiration tracking

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Passwords (we use API key-based authentication)
  • Payment information (no billing implemented yet)
  • Personal identification beyond email
  • Browsing history outside our service
  • Social media profiles or connections

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Service Provision

  • Authenticate API requests
  • Generate and manage access tokens
  • Enforce rate limits and access tiers
  • Provide technical support

2.2 Communication

  • Send account activation emails
  • Deliver API keys after activation
  • Notify about service updates or changes
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Send security alerts (API key resets, suspicious activity)

2.3 Security and Compliance

  • Monitor for abuse and Terms violations
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against security threats

2.4 Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns (aggregated, anonymized)
  • Improve API performance and reliability
  • Develop new features
  • Optimize user experience

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3.2 Service Providers

We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us:

  • Microsoft Azure: Email delivery via Microsoft Graph API
  • Railway: Application hosting and infrastructure
  • Database Hosting: Secure data storage (PostgreSQL)

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to:

  • Comply with legal process (subpoena, court order)
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect rights, property, or safety of Scientifics.io or others
  • Investigate potential violations
  • Respond to government requests

3.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you via email of any such change.

4. Data Security

4.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit
  • Hashing: API keys stored as bcrypt hashes (never plaintext)
  • JWT Tokens: Time-limited access tokens with HMAC signing
  • Rate Limiting: Protection against abuse and DDoS
  • Access Controls: Role-based permissions (PUBLIC, BASIC, PREMIUM, ADMIN)
  • Monitoring: Logging and alerting for suspicious activity

4.2 Your Responsibility

  • Keep your API key confidential
  • Use HTTPS when making API requests
  • Notify us immediately of suspected unauthorized access
  • Do not share your credentials

5. Data Retention

5.1 Active Accounts

  • Account data retained while account is active
  • API usage logs retained for 90 days
  • Authentication logs retained for 30 days

5.2 Inactive Accounts

  • Accounts inactive for 2 years may be deleted
  • Email notification sent 30 days before deletion
  • You can prevent deletion by logging in

5.3 Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by contacting support. Upon deletion:

  • Personal information deleted within 30 days
  • Aggregated usage statistics may be retained (anonymized)
  • Legal or compliance records may be retained as required

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Portability

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Export your account information
  • View your API usage history

6.2 Correction and Update

  • Update your email address
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Change account preferences

6.3 Deletion

  • Request account deletion
  • Revoke API access
  • Remove stored data

6.4 Opt-Out

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails (when implemented)
  • Disable non-essential notifications

7. Cookies and Tracking

7.1 Session Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication and user experience. These are essential for the service to function and expire after 1 hour.

7.2 Analytics (If Implemented)

We may use analytics tools to understand usage patterns. You can disable tracking through your browser settings.

7.3 Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Service provider cookies (if any) are used only for essential functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we discover we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using our service, you consent to such transfers.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
  • Right to deletion
  • Right to non-discrimination

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

European users have rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via:

  • Email notification to all users
  • Notice on our website
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top

Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
